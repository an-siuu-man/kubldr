# Landing page (/)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: KU students the week before their enrollment appointment. Action: try bldr as a guest (working button, signs in anonymously and opens /builder); secondary: create an account, log in. Proof: the permutation browser shown working on illustrative KU courses (labelled sample). Constraints: no "live/real-time" seat claims; no invented stats or testimonials; footer states bldr is not affiliated with KU.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every schedule is one sheet in a carbon-copy enrollment pad; flipping the top copy reveals the next combination. Refuses the centred-headline-over-framed-screenshot SaaS hero and its glows, icon cards and eyebrows.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper ground; triplicate copies in white, canary and pink stacked as offset planes; one crimson spot ink for all form rules, labels and display type; KU-blue ballpoint for fill-ins, class blocks and the handwritten aside. Archivo across its width axis (expanded black display, condensed form labels), Reenie Beanie only for ballpoint notes. Square corners, ruled hairlines, rubber stamps. Raises: stamped tally as record (matchbook); real stacked depth (multiplane); canary/pink own whole sections (cereal); previous schedule lingers as a carbon ghost (plankton).

STORY: The visitor sees 47 schedules fit, flips a few, understands bldr builds every combination, and starts as a guest.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width pad. Left column: form header, expanded crimson h1, one-line sub, signature box holding the guest button. Right: weekly grid with "03 of 47" counter and prev/next, rotated "47 FIT" stamp. Copy tabs peek below.

FORM: Registrar Carbon Copy, candidate 7 of 7. seed: none (unavailable)

Signature interaction: the pad auto-flips every few seconds (paused on hover/focus, off under reduced motion); the top sheet lifts away, blocks re-settle, the old layout stays as a fading dashed carbon impression.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
